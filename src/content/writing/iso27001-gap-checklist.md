A plain CSV checklist covering all 93 controls in ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, grouped by the
four 2022 themes (Organizational, People, Physical, Technological). Columns: control ID, control
name, implementation status, evidence reference, owner, target date, notes.

[Download the checklist (CSV)](/downloads/iso27001-annex-a-gap-checklist.csv)

This is a structural aid for tracking a gap assessment, not a substitute for the standard itself
— verify control wording against your licensed copy of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 before using this in a
real audit.
