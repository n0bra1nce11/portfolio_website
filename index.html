Why My Work Matters

Security isn't an abstraction to me — it's the difference between a brokerage firm passing an ISO 27001 audit and a client losing customer trust after a breach. I work both sides of that line: finding the holes as a penetration tester, and mapping the paperwork and controls that are supposed to close them as an IT auditor. That dual view keeps me honest about what actually reduces risk versus what just looks good on a checklist.
